7 Automation Rules — both Google Case Request & Automate Workflow
# Status Trigger Action ITH parity
1 Waiting for Customer 5 days — no reply Post public comment: automated reminder to customer ✅ Exists in ITH
2 Waiting for Customer 10 days — no reply Auto-transition → Resolved · post public comment ✅ Exists in ITH
3 Waiting for Approval 3 days — no approval Post reminder comment to approver ✅ Exists in ITH
4 Waiting for Approval 10 days — no approval Post escalation warning comment ✅ Exists in ITH
5 Waiting for Approval 13 days — no approval Auto-transition → Cancelled · post public comment ✅ Exists in ITH
6 On Hold Due date reached Auto-transition → In Progress · internal comment ✅ Exists in ITH
7 Resolved 7 days since resolution Auto-transition → Closed ✅ Exists in ITH
